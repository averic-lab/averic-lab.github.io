Parents living far away, a child living alone — you wonder if they’re okay today, but calling or visiting every day is hard.
When something happens, someone should know. Anbu delivers the word for you.

01

A daily, automatic check-in

A signal reaches your loved ones automatically at the same time each day, sensed naturally from that day's step count.

02

And quiet peace of mind

Even without reaching them directly, you can tell how they're doing — step by step. Reach out yourself only when it truly matters.

In the family app

This is how the alerts arrive.

From Normal to Urgent — Anbu shares the situation in five quiet steps.

  • NormalAll quiet today
  • CautionNo check-in yet, or no activity detected
  • WarningNo check-in or activity for days in a row
  • UrgentPlease check now
  • InfoSteps · Battery level
