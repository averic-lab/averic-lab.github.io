ANBU

Someone you love lives alone —
checking in every day isn't easy.

They're far away, and you wonder if they're okay today — but calling every day is hard.
When something happens, someone should know. Anbu delivers the word for you.

01

To send your check-in

No complicated setup. Only the way your check-in is sent differs slightly by phone.

  • Android Nothing to do — your check-in is sent automatically at the chosen time.
  • iPhone Once a day, a gentle reminder appears. Just tap it once — that's all it takes.
02

A daily, automatic check-in

A quiet signal reaches your loved ones at the same time each day, sensed naturally from movement and everyday use.

03

And quiet peace of mind

Even without reaching them directly, you can tell how they're doing — step by step. Reach out yourself only when it truly matters.

GRADED ALERTS

A gentle distance

Close at heart, but not every detail of your day.

  • Your location is shared only when you press [I need help] — reaching everyone connected to you.
