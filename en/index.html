OUR PROMISE

No names. No phone numbers. Nothing personal.

We protect the privacy of the people you care about. Connection happens through a single code we issue — nothing more.

  • Names, phone numbers, addresses are never stored on our servers.
  • Location is shared only when the [I need help] button is pressed by the user.
  • Personal data like call logs and photos is never collected. Ever.
